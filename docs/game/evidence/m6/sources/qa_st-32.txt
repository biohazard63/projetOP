Dracule Mihawk
Can I use this [On Play] effect to play a Character card
with a cost of 6 or more?
No, you cannot. This effect allows you to either play up
to 1 [Perona] with a cost of 5 or less from your hand,
or up to 1 Character card with a cost of 5 or less and
the <Slash> attribute from your hand.
