ST35-003
Karasu
Which player chooses what cards are trashed when this
card's [When Attacking] effect is activated?
The opponent of the player who used this [When
Attacking] effect will choose 1 card to trash from their
hand.
ST35-003
Karasu
If my opponent has 6 or less cards in their hand, can I
still use this card's [When Attacking] effect to trash 2
cards from the top of my deck?
Yes, you can. In this case, since your opponent does
not have 7 or more cards in their hand, your opponent
will not trash any cards from their hand.
